About

Hello, I'm Bruno.

I'm Bruno, a creative director living in Toronto. I work across branding, web development and motion graphics, and I have been passionately involved in those realms for well over a decade.

My work in digital, branding, experiential and creative strategy has activated campaigns and events for audiences in the automotive, entertainment, financial, CPG, and food and beverage industries.

I love collaborating with brilliant teams to make things that push the boundaries of creativity.

Away from the desk you will usually find me drawing, dabbling in 3D, contributing to open source projects and trying out new recipes.

I'm also the founder of Transparence, an AI powered creative studio.
