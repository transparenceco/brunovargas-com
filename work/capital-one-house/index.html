Capital One

Capital One House

A tech festival presence, and the party everyone talked about.

Client
Capital One
Disciplines
Design, Digital Signage, Graphic Design, Social Media Management

This key sponsorship at Elevate Tech Fest was a milestone for Capital One, establishing it as one of the leading financial institutions in the tech world.

We designed Capital One's visual presence for the whole conference, including social media, promotional goods, and vehicle and venue theming.

The Capital One House, the pinnacle of the event, popped up in downtown Toronto with a VIP cocktail making experience and a party open to Capital One cardholders. It delivered an unforgettable evening of diversity, technology and the arts, and left a lasting impression on attendees across all Elevate events.
