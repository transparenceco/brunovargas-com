Capital One Money Mindfulness

Measuring focus, one brainwave at a time, to talk about money.

Client
Capital One
Disciplines
Strategy, App Development, UX Design, Content Creation

Capital One partnered with Credit Canada and the Centre for Mindfulness Studies to talk with consumers about financial wellness during Credit Education Week.

We created an inviting indoor oasis where people could learn mindfulness practices and how that awareness can directly improve their financial health.

We developed a series of tools to start the conversation about credit, and hosted guided sessions that measured people's brainwaves to give them a report on their level of focus around money management.
