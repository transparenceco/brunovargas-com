A conference finale that left a court for everyone.

Disciplines

Every year, at the end of BMO's Regional Director conference, managers from around the world come together for a big act of corporate altruism in support of developing communities.

For this year's event in Toronto, we partnered with the Ontario Para Network (ONPARA) to celebrate the city's growing passion for basketball, and the para athletes and volunteers who make sure everybody has a place on the court.

The evening culminated in a team effort that remodelled ONPARA's facilities, unveiled a new practice facility and donated sport wheelchairs in Canada, the US and the UK.
